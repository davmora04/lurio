# Component handoff

## 1. Header
Desktop:
- wordmark left
- text navigation right
- dark pill CTA
- sticky, near-white background
Mobile:
- wordmark + menu icon
- full-screen or compact drawer; avoid overly app-like animation

## 2. Hero
Desktop: 52/48 split or editorial asymmetry.
Mobile: copy first, image second.
Required:
- eyebrow
- H1
- 2 short body paragraphs
- primary + secondary CTA
- hero image

## 3. Editorial statement
Large Playfair headline + concise supporting copy. Use generous whitespace.

## 4. Three/four principle cards
Keep corners nearly square.
Use text hierarchy over decorative icons.

## 5. Journey
Assess / Enter / Launch / Operate & Expand.
Avoid a thin horizontal “consulting process” line. Prefer substantial numbered panels or alternating editorial sections.

## 6. Open ecosystem block
High-importance differentiator. Consider dark ink background with amethyst accent.
Copy lead: “Your advisors, ours, or both.”

## 7. Why Lurio
Four capabilities:
- Client-side perspective
- Open orchestration
- Executive translation
- Hands-on accountability

## 8. CTA band
Large statement + one primary CTA.
No stock form embedded inside the hero.

## 9. Contact form
Recommended fields:
- Name
- Work email
- Company
- Role
- Company website
- What is driving Colombia for you? (select)
- Brief context
- Desired timing
Do not request sensitive information.

## 10. Insight card
Category / title / 1–2 sentence deck / date.
Photography or editorial graphic; no generic corporate icon.

## 11. Footer
Logo / tagline / contact / navigation / legal.
Keep compact and executive.

## Interaction principles
- Link underlines / small positional shifts
- 180–350ms transitions
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- No autoplay video by default
- No parallax that compromises readability
