# Content status / publication rules

## Approved brand language
- Keep Expansion Moving
- Keep your expansion moving.
- One expansion. One business objective. The right specialists.
- Your advisors, ours, or both.
- Client-side expansion partner
- Confidence / control as emotional outcomes

## Do NOT publish without validation
- Client logos
- Testimonials
- Number of expansions completed
- Number of countries served
- Quantified savings or ROI
- Claims such as “leading,” “best,” “#1,” “most trusted”
- Named partner firms unless permission is confirmed
- Any regulated-service claim implying Lurio itself provides legal/accounting/tax/recruiting/EOR services

## Launch geography
Sell Colombia capability today. Communicate LATAM as ambition, not as a fully built delivery footprint unless that changes.

## Contact fields to confirm before launch
- Public contact email
- Phone / WhatsApp, if used
- Founders' bios and headshots
- Legal entity name for privacy policy / terms
- Office address only if the founders want it public
