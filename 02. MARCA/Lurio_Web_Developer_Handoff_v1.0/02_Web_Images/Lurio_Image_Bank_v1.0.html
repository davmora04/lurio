LURIO · IMAGE BANK V1.0

Image sourcing bank for website and presentations.

This bank is designed for the Amethyst & Ink identity: distinctive sophistication, editorial architecture, business reality, subtle Colombia/LATAM context and practical operating environments.

How to use this bank

Use the rows below as curated image briefs and search links. Select images that feel premium, editorial, real and useful — not decorative. Final images should be checked for licensing, model/property releases when required, and crop suitability before publishing.

  • Unsplash: official license states images may be downloaded and used for free for commercial and non-commercial purposes, with no permission needed, though attribution is appreciated.
  • Pexels: official license states photos and videos can be downloaded and used for free; attribution is not required but appreciated; do not imply endorsement by people/brands in imagery.
  • Adobe Stock / Stocksy: paid-license options are recommended when Lurio needs tighter quality control, unique imagery, or broader usage assurance. Confirm the license type before publishing.

Selection rules

  • Prefer architectural framing, purposeful people, real business context and warm editorial light.
  • Avoid maps, globes, arrows, handshakes, flags, tourism clichés, call-center imagery and obvious SaaS/startup visuals.
  • For website hero images, prioritize negative space and strong crop behavior across desktop and mobile.
  • For presentations, use fewer but stronger images; do not overfill slides with decorative photography.
