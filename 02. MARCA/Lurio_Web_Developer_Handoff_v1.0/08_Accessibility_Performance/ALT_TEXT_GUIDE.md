# Image alt-text guide

`hero-architecture`: “Contemporary architectural passage framing a Colombian city and mountain horizon.”
If the image is purely decorative beside a headline, use alt="".

For authentic people photography:
Describe the action/context, not inferred identity or emotions.
Example: “Two business professionals discussing documents across a table.”

Avoid:
“Successful executive”
“Confident Latina leader”
or other inferred attributes.
